# Sales-analysis-dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a global e-commerce sales dataset using **Python** for data analysis and **Power BI** for interactive dashboard visualization. The goal is to identify sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, and profitability to support business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Identify top-selling products and categories
- Evaluate customer segments and payment methods
- Analyze regional sales and profit
- Study the relationship between discount and profit
- Build an interactive dashboard for business insights

---

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

The dashboard consists of four interactive pages:

### 1. Sales Overview
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Product Category
- Interactive Slicers

### 2. Product Analysis
- Top Selling Products
- Product Category Sales
- Product Category Profit
- Discount vs Profit Analysis

### 3. Customer Analysis
- Customer Segments
- Customers by Country
- Sales by Payment Method
- Sales by Customer Segment

### 4. Profit Analysis
- Profit by Region
- Profit by Payment Method
- Shipping Cost by Region
- Sales vs Profit Bubble Chart

## 📌 Key Insights

- Furniture generated the highest sales and profit.
- Credit Card was the most popular payment method.
- Europe and North America showed strong profitability.
- Higher discounts generally reduced profitability.
- Customer purchasing patterns varied across regions and categories.
